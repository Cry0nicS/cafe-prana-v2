# Order anything. It's all gluten-free and vegan.

A small café on Pasteurstraße in Prenzlauer Berg. I cook brunch and lunch myself, from organic plants, and nothing on the menu contains gluten. If you have coeliac disease, you can eat here without asking twice.

## My story

Founded by a yogini, nutritionist, foodie, nature lover, fitness trainer, health cook, and barista (...you get the persona...) who wanted to bring the holistic aspects of health and energy under one roof, Café Prana is a one-woman show that combines a welcoming atmosphere with high-quality sourcing.

Regional, seasonal vegetables meet nutritious pseudocereals and natural superfoods under the creative touch of the kitchen fairy. Whether you're grabbing a specialty coffee to go or settling into the cushions for brunch, it is a safe and open space awaiting you.

Prana's long-term goal is to implement even more sustainable practices and foster a sense of community and profound respect.

## Good to know

### Is everything on the menu gluten-free?

Yes, all of it. Every dish is prepared without gluten, so you can order without worrying.

### Do you take reservations?

Yes. Send a request with the [reservation form](/reservations) and I'll confirm by email, usually within 24 hours. You're also welcome to just drop by.

### Are pets allowed?

Not indoors, I'm sorry. Some guests have allergies or are afraid of animals, and a shared room with an open kitchen doesn't work with animals for hygiene reasons. Thank you for understanding.

### Do you offer take-away?

Yes, for most of the menu. Just ask when you order.
